# 1003 URLA — Borrower identity

Section I asks for the borrower's legal name, Social Security number (if collected), date of birth, citizenship, marital status, dependents, and contact information. Co-borrower data mirrors these items when a co-borrower applies jointly. Names must match government ID; phone and email are used for disclosures and servicing.

# 1003 URLA — Property and loan purpose

The subject property address, occupancy (primary/second home/investment), number of units, and property value are captured for collateral and underwriting. Loan purpose distinguishes purchase, refinance, construction, or other. Loan amount, product type, and term anchor pricing and eligibility.

# 1003 URLA — Employment and income

Borrowers declare employment status (employed, self-employed, retired, etc.), employer name, position, years in line of work, and monthly base, overtime, bonus, commission, military, and other income. Self-employed borrowers typically provide business name and ownership percentage. Income must be stated as the borrower described it on the call.

# 1003 URLA — Assets and liabilities

Liquid assets (checking, savings, retirement), gifts, and other credits are listed. Liabilities include installment loans, revolving accounts, child support, and other recurring obligations. Extract only figures and descriptions explicitly stated in the conversation.

# 1003 URLA — Loan details

Common fields include loan term in years or months, amortization type, lien position, and down payment or equity from borrower funds. Down payment may be expressed as a dollar amount or percentage of purchase price when clearly tied to the transaction.

# 1003 URLA — Credit and declarations

Credit score may be borrower-reported or from a tri-merge pull; capture the number and bureau if mentioned. Declarations cover occupancy intent, outstanding judgments, bankruptcy, foreclosure, and other legal/financial events—extract only when the borrower affirms or denies on the call.

# 1003 URLA — Demographics (Government Monitoring)

Ethnicity, sex, and race information may be collected under HMDA/GMI rules when provided voluntarily on the call. Do not infer protected characteristics; only extract when explicitly stated.

# 1003 URLA — Military service

Current or former military service and type of service may affect VA eligibility and fees. Capture branch, years of service, or discharge status only when clearly mentioned.
